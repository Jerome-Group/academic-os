# GLOSSARY

Subject terms as this module uses them. Organisational language lives in the module's
`CONTEXT.md`, never here.

- **Partial derivative** — the single-variable derivative of a trace: hold every other
  variable fixed and differentiate along the remaining one.
- **Differentiable (multivariable)** — the increment form holds: the tangent plane is
  first-order accurate. Strictly stronger than both partials existing.
- **Clairaut's theorem** — mixed partials commute wherever both are continuous.
