# Contribution and AI Use

The Owner authors the mathematics and every assessed artifact. Assistance may help locate sources,
explain ideas, test examples, compile files and critique work. Material enters Research or a
Deliverable only after the Owner has reconstructed or rewritten it, verified its citations and
chosen to adopt it.

Record material assistance that changes an adopted artifact. For GenAI, include tool, version,
purpose and manner of use. Mechanical operations with no intellectual effect need no row.

## External-release gate

Before research data collection or external release, check and cite the applicable programme,
GenAI, data/confidentiality, intellectual-property, human-subject and receiving-party rules. Record
`not applicable` with evidence; unresolved ownership, confidentiality or approval parks release.
