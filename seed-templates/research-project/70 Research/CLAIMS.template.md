# Claims

Claims the project may rely on. Each entry names its status, Source-register IDs and precise
locators, and the Owner-authored Research artifact that establishes or checks it.

Copy this interface for a real Claim; the fenced copy is not an entry:

```markdown
## claim-key — Short statement

- Status: candidate
- Statement and scope:
- Sources and precise locators:
- Owner-authored check:
- Dependencies or superseding Claim:
```

Statuses: `candidate`, `checked`, `refuted`, `superseded`.
