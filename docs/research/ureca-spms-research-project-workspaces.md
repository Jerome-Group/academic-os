# URECA and SPMS research-project workspace evidence

Research checked on 2026-08-31 for the research-project contract. This note records what the
official sources establish, what they leave unresolved, and which workspace decision each finding
supports. It is programme research, not a copy of any student's project, registration or work.

## Source order

1. The current URECA student page and the linked URECA course outline govern URECA participation
   and assessment.
2. A project brief and the supervising professor govern one project's mathematical scope and
   working practice.
3. SPMS Mathematics FYP pages inform a reusable mathematics-research workspace only where they
   describe a research practice rather than an FYP requirement.
4. Older, undated or unversioned NTU pages are corroboration. They do not settle a current
   academic-year date against a current student page or authenticated notice.

## What current URECA guidance establishes

The [URECA course outline](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/media/docs/default-source/ureca-programme/ureca-obtl-course-outline--01-10-2024.pdf?sfvrsn=7c53097b_25)
describes an eleven-month August-to-June programme of independent, supervised and open-ended
research. Its learning outcomes include defining a research problem, reviewing literature,
planning the work, applying an appropriate method, analysing results, reflecting and communicating
the result. The supervisor recommends the field-specific reading list and provides formative
feedback.

The [current-student guidance](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/undergraduate-research-experience-on-campus-%28ureca%29/current-students)
requires reasonable-frequency supervisor meetings, individual work products, workshops with
quizzes, and four deliverables: abstract, poster, research paper and reflection. Its assessment
table gives these standing windows and limits:

| Item | Standing window or period | Stated size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research-integrity learning | by end December in the course-outline schedule | programme modules |
| Abstract | end February | at most 250 words |
| Poster | end March | one exhibit-able poster |
| Research paper | end June | 4–12 pages |
| Reflection | end June | at most 250 words |
| Research experience | throughout, assessed at end June | supervisor assessment |

The same sources make academic integrity operational: the student owns the work, outside sources
are referenced, collaboration is acknowledged, and the individual deliverables remain individually
authored. A workspace therefore needs source provenance and a durable contribution/AI-use record,
while leaving mathematical prose and assessed artifacts to the student.

The current page also says a supervisor-rejected poster or paper is resubmitted within three days,
and gives the paper a four-percent per-source Turnitin similarity ceiling. It records the URECA
journal-publication reward as discontinued for students enrolled from AY2026-27. These are URECA
programme facts to retain in a private Profile/checklist; none changes the generic tree.

## What SPMS FYP guidance contributes

The [SPMS Mathematics FYP page](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms/about-us/mathematics/undergrad/fyp)
describes supervised mathematics research culminating in written and oral communication. The
[SPMS Mathematics FAQ](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms/about-us/mathematics/undergrad/faqs) frames a
proposal around motivation, the fundamental problem and difficulty, and a plan or method; its
presentation guidance calls for literature, methods, proof or experiment outlines, results and
possible extensions. Those are useful generic research interfaces: questions, sources,
mathematical work, experiments and deliverables.

The [AY2026-27 MH4916 course outline](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/librariesprovider123/obtl/mas/updated-obtl/mh4916-spms-mas-outcomes-based-teaching-and-learning-document-%28obtl%29.pdf?sfvrsn=64c71e4c_1)
adds a field-specific supervisor-recommended literature review and a weekly schedule agreed between
student and supervisor. Its FYP assessment is a progress report, presentation and thesis with
continuous supervisor feedback. The agreed cadence supports durable meeting notes; its three
outputs remain FYP-profile structure.

FYP-specific progress reports, presentations, thesis lengths, assessment weights, DR-NTU upload
and embargo handling are profile requirements, not universal research-project structure. A future
FYP profile may declare them without changing the universal contract.

## Research-policy gates

The [NTU Research Integrity Policy](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/ntu-research-integrity-policy)
requires honest, accountable work and points research data to the Research Data Policy. The
[Research Data Policy](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/ntu-research-data-policy) requires systematic
management, protection from unauthorised access and consideration of legal, ethical, commercial,
non-disclosure and proprietary constraints before sharing. A pure-mathematics project may generate
no governed research dataset, but the Project Profile must say so rather than assuming the policy
away.

NTU's [GenAI policy for research](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/resources/use-of-gai-in-research),
last reviewed 7 November 2025, requires responsible, accountable and transparent use; use beyond
basic spelling/grammar is acknowledged with tool, version, purpose and manner of use. It makes the
human author responsible for accuracy, bars GenAI authorship, protects confidential/sensitive data
from external tools and defers to the stricter receiving-party rule. This supports both the
adoption log and an external-release check.

The [NTU Intellectual Property Policy](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/legal-and-secretarial-office/policy/ntu-ip-policy.pdf?sfvrsn=434fa79b_1),
version 5 effective 19 January 2026, distinguishes student-owned work from University-owned IP,
requires disclosure of potentially commercial University IP, protects confidential invention
information and requires permission for third-party content. The workspace therefore does not
promise that a file is safe to publish merely because the Owner wrote it.

NTU's [student human-subject guidance](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/research/research-integrity-office/institutional-review-board/guidelines/students-research)
applies when a project uses participants, interviews, surveys, observations, identifiable private
information or other human interaction. Coursework research such as URECA routes ethics review
through the School ERC or NTU-IRB as applicable, before the study begins. This pure-mathematics
design expects that branch to be not applicable, while keeping it as an explicit Profile gate.

## Conflicts and gaps

- The public current-student page gives month-end windows and uses images for some important-date
  details. Its accessible text does not bind those images to a named AY2026-27 schedule. Older
  static submission guides expose exact dates for earlier years. A day inferred by reusing an old
  year or reading an unversioned image is therefore provisional, not a confirmed current deadline.
- The same current-student page says to attend three workshops, while its assessment block labels
  Workshops 1 and 2 for first-time students and Workshops 3 and 4 for returning students. The
  authenticated workshop schedule must settle what applies to one student; the workspace records
  no invented count.
- Public pages do not establish one student's exact registration identity, project brief,
  supervisor agreement, meeting cadence, recommended readings or submission templates. Those enter
  a private Project Profile or Source Register only from Owner-supplied or authenticated evidence.
- The course outline says the supervisor recommends field-specific readings. A generic seed can
  provide a staged source register, but it cannot name a project's authoritative reading list.
- URECA mentions an NTULearn course site for programme learning. No importer or mirror is thereby
  established. An importer root appears only when a real integration is configured.

## Resulting design

| Evidence | Workspace decision |
| --- | --- |
| Literature review precedes a defined problem | Separate source materials, reading work and a research map |
| Open-ended work under supervision | Questions and claims remain revisable; meetings have durable notes and follow-ups |
| Pure mathematics communicates proofs and may use examples or computation | Separate Mathematics and Experiments areas under one Research workspace |
| Four URECA deliverables | The URECA profile adds Abstract, Poster, Paper and Reflection workspaces |
| Individual authorship and source attribution | Owner authors; agents explain, locate, check and critique; contributions and adopted help are recorded |
| GenAI, data, IP and ethics policy | Check confidentiality, disclosure, ownership and applicable approval before external release or data collection |
| Dates vary by programme and academic year | Google Calendar owns confirmed deadlines; unresolved windows stay explicit gaps until verified |
| Research tasks differ from deadlines | A dedicated Google Tasks list owns work and a project register mirrors it; Calendar milestones own deadlines |
| FYP has a similar method but different outputs | One research-project aggregate with profile-derived deliverables, not one URECA- or FYP-shaped universal tree |
| No current importer exists | The universal seed contains no NTULearn directory |
