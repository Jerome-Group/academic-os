# 48. Generated Module activities share one temporal owner

Date: 2026-11-06

Status: Accepted for generated candidates only

## Context

Snapshot schemas cannot establish whether a new learning record legally follows its predecessor.
Procedures also distinguish requested scope, official coverage, unaided evidence, artifact readiness
and provider outcomes. A caller's attributed judgment cannot manufacture academic truth or consent.

The Module-data draft is unmerged and undeployed. Its Generation 2 role set already holds the facts
this protocol needs. Live Module migration and teaching invocation remain separately owned.

## Decision

Add the optional protocol and closeout fields to the existing draft. Keep all 18 roles and preserve
old readability. Direct initial enablement requires empty history. Importing old records requires a
protocol-free baseline followed by a preservation-only adoption transition.

Controls owns exact procedure and preference recognition, evidence requirements, coverage,
supersession and temporal legality. Its validator consumes native observations and typed request
context without importing publisher runtime. The existing publisher exposes pure complete-bundle
validation and invokes that same temporal validator after prior-request handling and owned
expected-head resolution. One permanent owner retains mutation and recovery authority.

Use portable preparations and frozen plans instead of a workflow database. Retained descriptors
omit the predecessor closure and full preparation respectively, avoiding recursive packet retention.
Close verifies the original plan and preserves its exact bytes for durable publisher replay.

Exact reviewed full procedure bytes select executable consequences. Shared preference identities
assigned by the new registry are distinct from original Markdown metadata. Unknown local meanings
remain explicit prerequisites. Every real revision profile parks native note or reuse verification
until its owner can admit and verify the artifact.

## Consequences

Raw publication cannot bypass enabled history or evidence rules. Matching completed requests still
replay before fresh head and transition checks. Existing unknown facts remain unknown, and old
records stay intact. Preparations can grow with history and refuse at the complete packet limit.
Schema-valid snapshots, passing local fixtures and published control evidence establish neither
academic truth nor a completed real teaching session.
