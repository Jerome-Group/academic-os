# Research work begins in a meeting and promotion is explicit

> Supersedes ADR-0024 in part for the universal research tree and where ordinary work lives.

Ordinary Research-project work lives in one supervisor meeting. Its sources, Learning, Exercises
and session records stay together under `20 Supervisor Meetings/YYYY-MM-DD Topic/`. A completed
session may promote nothing. Work enters `70 Research` only when the Owner adopts it explicitly.

## Why

The first contract split reading, mathematics and experiments into parallel Research homes. The
active project instead organizes work around the meeting that supplied its evidence and direction.
The old tree separated an artifact from the sources, guidance and records needed to interpret it,
and treated Research as the default destination before adoption had happened.

The meeting is a durable evidence boundary. Calendar owns a confirmed date, `SCHEDULE.md` indexes
the local folders, and `Meeting.md` owns attribution and settlement. Stable Source, Question and
Research-map identities connect work across meetings without moving its history.

## Consequences

Contract v2 requires dated meeting containers and moves shared templates to `60 Templates`.
`70 Research` contains optional Concepts, Research Notes and promotion records. Source-register
rows distinguish project and meeting storage and describe exact copies or extracts. Research-map
rows point to meeting work and promoted outputs.

Shared routers, procedures and blank Markdown templates can be refreshed for one configured
project after preview and verified backup. Project context, meeting prose, mathematics, records,
preferences and locally adapted LaTeX remain project-owned. A v1 Definition requires an explicit
migration whose projected state is reviewed before the Definition is written last.
